# Lockwood IT Solutions — decisions required before public launch

The website is intentionally conservative where the business model is not yet finalized. Confirm these items before production publication or before adding stronger claims.

## Business model

- Final target client range and any minimum user/device commitment
- Staffed support hours and after-hours arrangements
- Geographic limits for included on-site service
- Whether projects are accepted without a managed-services relationship
- Whether residential work is referral-only, a separate brand, or discontinued

## Service delivery

- Remote monitoring and management platform
- Endpoint protection and response platform
- Email security and DNS/web-filtering platforms
- Backup platforms and restore-testing cadence
- Ticketing, documentation, and password-management systems
- Microsoft licensing and billing responsibility
- Hardware procurement and warranty process
- Escalation partners for specialized security or field work

## Commercial terms

- Onboarding or stabilization fees
- Minimum monthly commitment
- Included and excluded labor
- On-site and after-hours rates
- Contract term, renewal, cancellation, and annual adjustment terms
- Service-level definitions and response targets

## Sales and operations

- Primary conversion path: web form, phone, Microsoft Bookings, or a combination
- Lead follow-up owner and workflow
- Proposal and agreement templates
- Customer support intake process
- Billing and accounting workflow

## Claims and evidence

Do not publish the following unless supported by current records and approved for publication:

- Customer counts or device counts
- Uptime, patch, response-time, or security metrics
- “24/7” coverage
- “Zero incidents” or breach-prevention guarantees
- HIPAA, PCI, or other compliance claims
- Customer names, logos, testimonials, or case studies
- Certifications that are expired or not held by the business/person identified

## Legal and privacy

- Review the website Privacy Policy and Website Terms
- Confirm SendGrid sender-domain authentication
- Confirm how inquiry emails and business records are retained
- Confirm whether analytics, scheduling, chat, or marketing tools will be added
- Update privacy disclosures before adding any new tracking or third-party embeds
